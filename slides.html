SLIDE 06 / 07 • JUDGE QUESTION #3: LIMITATIONS & NEXT STEPS SCIENTIFIC RIGOR & ROADMAP

What Doesn't Work Yet & What We Would Build Next

Current Limitations (What Doesn't Work Yet)

  • Synthetic 450-Sol Crew Trajectories: No human has flown a 450-Sol Mars mission yet; our 450-Sol crew vitals are synthetic (seeded & literature-calibrated), whereas our real NASA Inspiration4 blood validation covers a 3-day orbital mission with small pre-flight sample size (n = 3 timepoints: L-92, L-44, L-3).
  • Single-Compartment Vitamin Pools: Models water-soluble vitamins (C, B1) and serum 25(OH)D as 1st-order compartments; does not yet model multi-compartment tissue storage or gut microbiome malabsorption under cosmic radiation (GCR).
  • Software-Simulated IoT Valves: Regolith VWC% probes and solenoid valves are currently software-emulated rather than wired to physical hardware probes.

Next Steps (If Given More Time)

  • Physical Hardware-in-the-Loop at ADAU Ganja Phytotron: Connect physical ESP32/STM32 capacitive soil moisture & EC salinity sensors inside MGS-1 regolith simulant trays growing real GHM-01 microgreens to stream live MQTT telemetry into FastAPI.
  • GCR Radiation + Thermal Coupled Kinetics: Add Galactic Cosmic Ray (GCR) ionizing dose terms to the Arrhenius rate constant k(T, D_rad) for lipid peroxidation and folate/B12 decay.
  • Closed-Loop Wearable Integration: Stream live smartwatch PPG heart-rate variability (HRV) and actigraphy sleep stages directly into the causal baseline z-score engine.
