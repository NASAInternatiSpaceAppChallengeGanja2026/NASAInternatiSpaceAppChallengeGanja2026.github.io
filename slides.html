SLIDE 02 / 07 • PROBLEM STATEMENT & IMPACT MARS SOL-450 HUMAN SURVIVAL BOTTLENECK

Calories Survive Storage. Micronutrients & Crew Physiology Do Not.

On a 450-to-1000 Sol Mars mission, astronauts have zero Earth resupply. Prepackaged rations undergo continuous chemical decay governed by Arrhenius thermodynamics. A single cooling-loop failure (+29.5°C) silently destroys Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C) and Thiamine (B1), triggering scurvy, cognitive decline, and metabolic drift long before caloric stores run out.

32–83% Vit C Loss
Invisible Shelf-Life Decay

Published studies: 32–83% vitamin C loss after 3 years at 21°C (Cooper 2017); 3–55% thiamine (B1) retention after 720 days at 20°C (Goulette 2020). Calories can look sufficient while vitamins fall.

Subclinical Drift
Population Ranges Miss Early Risk

Standard clinical reference ranges are too wide to catch an individual astronaut's early physiological drift until acute deficiency symptoms appear.

NASA Data Used in NurtureAISpace

1. NASA OSDR (Open Science Data Repository)

OSD-569 (CBC) & OSD-575 (CMP) — Real Inspiration4 Blood Tests

553 CBC rows and 531 CMP rows for 4 crew. Each person is compared with their own 3 pre-flight tests. At R+1, MCV was lower than all 3 of their own pre-flight values in all 4 crew, still inside the 80–100 fL lab range. Limits: 4 people, 3 baseline tests, 3-day mission.

2. NASA JSC 2005 & Space Food Studies

NASA JSC 2005 Nutrition Requirements (NTRS 20200001703)

Vitamin C 90 mg and thiamine 1.2 mg daily targets. Food vitamin retention is compared with published endpoints (Cooper 2017, Goulette 2020): only 7 of 12 agree with our synthetic catalog.

3. ISS Vitamin D (NASA NTRS)

Rice/Zwart/Smith ISS Vitamin D (NTRS 20080026180)

Basis of the first-order serum 25(OH)D decline model. Energy and protein targets in the optimizer are assumed.

4. NASA BVAD REV1

Baseline Values and Assumptions Document (NTRS 20180001338)

Crew rates per person-day: 0.816 kg O₂, 1.04 kg CO₂, 2.50 kg potable and 7.32 kg hygiene water. The rest of the life-support loop is synthetic.

SLIDE 06 / 07 • JUDGE QUESTION #3: LIMITATIONS & NEXT STEPS SCIENTIFIC RIGOR & ROADMAP

What Doesn't Work Yet & What We Would Build Next

Current Limitations (What Doesn't Work Yet)

  • Synthetic crew: No human has flown a 450-Sol Mars mission, so our crew trajectories are synthetic. The real Inspiration4 data has 4 people, 3 baseline tests and a 3-day mission. Single flags are mostly noise (26% flagged at L-3 vs 20% at R+1).
  • Single-Compartment Vitamin Pools: Models water-soluble vitamins (C, B1) and serum 25(OH)D as 1st-order compartments; does not yet model multi-compartment tissue storage or gut microbiome malabsorption under cosmic radiation (GCR).
  • Literature check: only 7 of 12 published retention endpoints agree with our synthetic catalog (RMSE about 27 percentage points). Soil moisture controller is software-simulated.

Next Steps (If Given More Time)

  • Hardware test: Try real moisture sensors on a small crop bed.
  • GCR Radiation + Thermal Coupled Kinetics: Add Galactic Cosmic Ray (GCR) ionizing dose terms to the Arrhenius rate constant k(T, D_rad) for lipid peroxidation and folate/B12 decay.
  • Closed-Loop Wearable Integration: Stream live smartwatch PPG heart-rate variability (HRV) and actigraphy sleep stages directly into the causal baseline z-score engine.
