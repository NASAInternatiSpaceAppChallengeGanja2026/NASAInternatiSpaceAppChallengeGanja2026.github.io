1. Arrhenius Kinetic Law

Reaction rate constant \( k \) as a function of absolute temperature \( T \):

k = A · exp( -E_a / (R · T) )

Where \( A \) is the pre-exponential frequency factor, \( E_a \) is the activation energy (\( ext{kJ/mol} \)), \( R = 8.314462618 ext{ J/(mol}\cdot ext{K)} \) is the ideal gas constant, and \( T \) is storage temperature in Kelvin.

2. Constrained Linear Programming

Minimize objective cost (payload stowage mass or chilling energy):

min cᵀx subject to A_ub · x ≤ b_ub, x ≥ 0

Subject to NASA STD-3001 bounds: Daily energy ≥ 2,800 kcal, Protein ≥ 85g, Thiamine ≥ 1.2mg, Ascorbic acid ≥ 90mg, Rehydration water ≤ 2.5 L/day. Solved with SciPy HiGHS simplex.

3. N-BLSS Light Use Efficiency (LUE) (LUE)

Daily biomass yield under Martian controlled environment:

Y_biomass = Area · β_cultivar · √( DLI_actual / DLI_nominal )

Where \( ext{DLI} = ( ext{PPFD} \cdot ext{Photoperiod} \cdot 3600) / 10^6 \). Calibrated with ADAU Ganja phytotron microgreen trials, yielding 148 mg/100g Vitamin C.

[ClO4⁻](t) = [ClO4⁻]₀ · exp( -k_remediation · t )
