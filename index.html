1. Arrhenius Kinetic Law

Reaction rate constant k as a function of absolute temperature T:

k = A · exp( -E_a / (R · T) )

Where A is the pre-exponential frequency factor, E_a is activation energy (kJ/mol), R = 8.314 J/(mol·K), and T is storage temperature in Kelvin.

2. Constrained Linear Programming

Minimize objective cost (payload stowage mass or chilling energy):

min cᵀx subject to A_ub · x ≤ b_ub, x ≥ 0

Subject to NASA STD-3001 bounds: Daily energy ≥ 2,800 kcal, Protein ≥ 85g, Thiamine ≥ 1.2mg, Ascorbic acid ≥ 90mg, Rehydration water ≤ 2.5 L/day.

3. N-BLSS Light Use Efficiency (LUE)

Daily biomass yield under Martian controlled environment:

Y_biomass = Area · β_cultivar · √( DLI_actual / DLI_nominal )

Where DLI = (PPFD · Photoperiod · 3600) / 10⁶. Calibrated with phytotron microgreen trials yielding 148 mg/100g Vitamin C.
