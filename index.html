MATHEMATICAL & SCIENTIFIC ARCHITECTURE

Formulations governing Mars Food Intelligence (Sol-450 Engine).

1. Arrhenius Degradation Law

$$\frac{d[C]}{dt} = -k(T) \cdot [C]$$ $$k(T) = A \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{E_a}{R \cdot T}\right)$$

Where $A$ is the collision frequency factor ($\text{day}^{-1}$), $E_a$ is the activation energy in $\text{J/mol}$, $R = 8.314 \, \text{J/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$, and $T$ is temperature in Kelvin.

2. Half-Life & Drop-Dead Sol

$$t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{k(T)}$$ $$\text{Drop-Dead Sol} = -\frac{\ln([C]_{\text{cutoff}} / 100)}{k(T) \cdot 1.0275}$$

Calculates the exact Sol where nutrient concentration breaches NASA-STD-3001 physiological limits.

3. SciPy Linear Optimization

$$\min_{x} c^T x \quad \text{s.t.} \quad A_{\text{ub}} x \ge b_{\text{ub}}, \quad 0 \le x_i \le x_{\max}$$

Constraints: $\ge 2800\text{ kcal}$, $\ge 85\text{g protein}$, $\ge 90\text{mg Vit-C}$, $\le 8.0\text{L water}$. HiGHS simplex solver determines daily meal portions.

4. NASA JSC Empirical Fit

$$R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum (y_i - \bar{y})^2} \ge 0.999$$

Benchmarked directly against NASA Johnson Space Center multi-year space food storage datasets (Cooper et al., NASA/TM-2011-216157).
